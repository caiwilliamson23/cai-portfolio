About Cassius Design Studio

Cassius Design Studio is a creative design business focused on turning ideas into bold, memorable visuals. Founded by Cai Williamson, the studio specializes in branding, cover art, promotional graphics, and web design for artists, music producers, entrepreneurs, and growing businesses. Every project is designed to reflect the client’s unique identity while helping them connect with their audience.

At Cassius Design Studio, the goal is to create more than something that simply looks good. Each design combines creativity, purpose, and attention to detail to tell a story and leave a lasting impression. Whether a client is developing a new brand, releasing a music project, or building an online presence, Cassius Design Studio works closely with them to bring their vision to life.
